Unit Dispatch

ResQ-AI generates recommendations, classifications, and summaries. It does NOT make autonomous critical decisions. Every AI-generated assessment must be reviewed and authorized by a human dispatcher.

Fairness & Bias Mitigation

The system is audited to process reports uniformly regardless of geographic location or language. We acknowledge digital divide biases in crowdsourced reporting; human operators are trained to allocate resources equitably despite data sparsity in lower-income sectors.

Transparency & Explainability

AI decisions are strictly accompanied by a readable explanation. The RAG module explicitly cites its exact sources (e.g., HAZMAT ERG pages) for emergency guidance, eliminating opaque "black-box" directives.

Privacy & Security

User location data is captured exclusively with active consent during reporting. Media uploads are processed securely in memory and flushed. Personal identifiable information (PII) is automatically redacted during AI entity extraction.

Hallucination Defense

Using strict prompt templates and IBM Granite Guardian capabilities, the AI is explicitly instructed to refuse out-of-domain medical diagnoses and to output "Unknown" when incident data is insufficient, preventing dangerous fabricated advice.
