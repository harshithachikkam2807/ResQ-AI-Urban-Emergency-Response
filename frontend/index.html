Why This Matters

The Golden Hour

Delayed manual triage costs lives. AI accelerates initial classification, ensuring rapid deployment within the critical first hour of trauma.

Information Fragmentation

During chaotic events, incoming data is noisy. IBM Granite extracts structured entities (locations, casualties) from raw citizen input.

Protocol Adherence

RAG retrieves exact municipal SOPs (e.g., HAZMAT isolation zones) preventing dispatcher hallucination and ensuring standardized response.
